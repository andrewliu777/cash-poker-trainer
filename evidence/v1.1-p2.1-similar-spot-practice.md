# P2.1 Similar spot practice — 2026-09-30

## Scope and data boundary

A completed free-table hand can supply a marked human action in replay. The first supported spot is a first-in preflop decision with an unopened pot and zero or more earlier bot folds. The extractor checks the completed event stream, action order, full blinds, dealt hero cards, funded seats, chip unit, current bot style version, pot, and amount to call. Other marks remain review notes and do not enable practice.

Each practice hand recreates the source seats, starting stacks, blinds, dealer position, and ordered prior folds. It resets those stacks before every sample, shuffles a fresh full deck from a recorded 32-bit seed, and lets normal bots decide after the hero's action. Its local history records `similar_sample`, source hand/decision, filters, sample ID, ordinal, reset index, and deck seed. The seed reproduces the deal, not later bot randomness. Seat-view backup and hand text omit the seed: with the public shuffle algorithm it would reveal unrevealed cards. Practice has a distinct saved-game key and history session; the free table's saved continuation and net result are not overwritten. No original-hand branch or strategy/GTO score is offered.

## Limits

The exercise does not preserve the source hero cards, unknown opponent cards, future board, old deck, or old bot decisions. Current support excludes postflop spots, opened pots, short blind posts, unfunded seats, legacy chip units, and older bot style versions. `resetIndex` and sample identity record each fresh deal; they do not claim exact replay of the original hand. The browser check covered two-seat UI play, refresh, export, and return; supported multi-seat fold-prefix extraction is covered by unit tests rather than a browser run.
