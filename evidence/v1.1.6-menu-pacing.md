# v1.1.6 menu and fold pacing — 2026-09-30

## Change

The Table menu now shows Bot pace (Natural / Instant) and Sound (On / Off) as visible two-choice controls. Rows that open a view or enter another play mode have a right arrow. Preference storage keys remain unchanged.

Instant pace now finishes the remaining hand quickly after the only human folds: bot decisions remain immediate, notifications and payout use the fast path, and an all-in board runout skips the remaining street pauses. The separate Fast Forward button stays hidden during this automatic finish. Natural pace retains the manual Fast Forward choice after a fold.
