# v0.3 O1 information and action boundary — 2026-09-29

The legacy bot now receives a fresh seat observation instead of the full mutable game state. Only the acting seat's hole cards are present. Other hole cards, future cards, all-knowing win probabilities, UI visibility flags, and other agents' internal plans are absent. The bot's own memory is copied into and back out of the adapter.

The browser's human and bot turns, and the browserless engine runner, now use one action ticket per turn. A ticket identifies hand, decision, and seat. An accepted action consumes the ticket. Raise requests at this boundary use `raiseTo`, the total amount invested on the current street; the existing slider and bot amount requests are converted at their adapters. The engine still validates chip precision, minimum raise, action reopening, and stack cap. Invalid actions leave the ticket available for correction. Old hand, old decision, wrong seat, and duplicate submissions are rejected.
