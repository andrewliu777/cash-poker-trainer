# D0.4 Six-way NLHE postflop solver survey — 2026-09-30

## Question and decision

The requested coverage is a **six-seat NLHE cash table with as many as six players still active at a flop/turn/river decision**, not merely six seats preflop followed by a heads-up postflop tree. This survey checks product claims and published interfaces; it does not certify a vendor's solver or import its output into the trainer.

**Result: conditional research Go, integration No-Go today.** Commercial desktop engines including MonkerSolver, IkaSolver, and the newer Prism Solver claim postflop solves with six active players. No tested, licensed path currently connects any of them to this static trainer and returns per-combo action EV for an exact six-way hand. GTO Wizard AI and Simple 3-Way stop at three-player postflop. The existing MIT browser candidate is heads-up only. A six-way *scenario* may be solvable with a restricted tree and enough hardware, but an automatic, accurate score for every arbitrary six-way hand is unproven.

No free, ready-to-integrate arbitrary six-active-player postflop grader was confirmed in this survey; that is a scoped research finding, not a claim that none can exist.

## Why six-way coverage is hard

1. **Tree and compute:** Six independent ranges, action branches, future boards, stack differences and side pots multiply state size. The commercial pages establish configurable player counts, not bounded latency or RAM for our arbitrary 100bb six-way spots. Sparse betting trees and card bucketing can make a solve feasible but change the modeled game. The observed action must be present exactly in the tree before displaying a numerical EV loss.
2. **Ranges:** A hand history does not reveal five opponents' ranges. Each range has to be estimated from the preflop line and updated through later actions; revealed showdown cards cannot be treated as knowledge at earlier decisions. A displayed EV difference is conditional on those ranges, stack/pot state, rake=0 and action tree.
3. **Multi-player quality:** A stable strategy is not a proof of a near-equilibrium result. GTO Wizard's [three-way benchmark note](https://blog.gtowizard.com/gto_wizard_ai_3_way_benchmarks/) explains that multiplayer solution quality needs different checks from two-player zero-sum exploitability. In particular, IkaSolver's volatility threshold alone is insufficient as a quality certificate, and MonkerSolver's FAQ disclaims full-strategy exploitability measurement.
4. **Integration:** `file://` JavaScript cannot run a native desktop CLI. A licensed local companion or an authorized remote API would be needed for an automated commercial solve. No public permission to embed Monker/Ika binaries or automatically extract their postflop output has been verified. Do not scrape GTO Wizard or reverse engineer proprietary files as an integration shortcut.

## Next gate, ordered by smallest useful proof

1. Keep D1 decision snapshots **player-count neutral**: active seats (2–6), board, pot, each stack/contribution, side-pot eligibility, action line, hero combo, and explicit missing-range reasons. Do not collapse a six-way state into a heads-up one.
2. Validate one six-active-player **river** spot, then turn and flop, first with a small exact action tree. For each street, record six non-empty starting ranges and their provenance; use a no-rake trainer hand with no side pot for the first fixture. Compare the selected action and all *modeled* legal alternatives at the hero combo, with the recorded action size present exactly in the tree.
3. Request documented input/output and licensing terms from a vendor **only if the user authorizes outreach**. For Ika, confirm `.tree` construction and lawful parsing/export of per-combo postflop EV from `.iks`; for Monker, confirm any supported automation/export interface; for Prism, confirm whether its JSON report carries node-specific per-combo action EV and whether the licence permits private trainer use. Independently measure RAM, wall time and solution stability on a real six-way 20bb and 100bb fixture. Precomputed viewers are insufficient for this gate.
4. Only after a matching fixture passes should the app offer six-way conditional EV assessment. Until then, show `unavailable` for six-way solver grading while retaining snapshots and hand text for manual study. No solver, licensed result, or runtime was installed in this investigation.
