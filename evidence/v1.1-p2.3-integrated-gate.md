The six-case regression matrix derived supported spots from both 3-seat and 6-seat completed hands at 20, 100 and 200 BB. Every case preserved seat count, each starting stack, prior fold order, pot and call amount. The existing seeded-shuffle test covers repeatability of a recorded deal seed.
