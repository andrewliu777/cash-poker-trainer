# v1.1.4 session and action feedback — 2026-09-30

## Scope

Cash-table seat badges show the last action and its amount in place of the bot name until the next street. New sessions have timestamp-prefixed unique IDs; the review selector shows that date and time in local format. The Table menu offers Natural (randomized 2–4 second bot delay) and Instant (zero bot queue delay); the choice persists locally.
