# v0.1 rules core acceptance

Date: 2026-09-29. Scope: `M1` integer chip units and `R1` betting legality. The table is still the upstream tournament UI; cash-session behavior belongs to v0.2.

## Contracts

- `chipUnit` is the smallest integer amount that can be wagered. It defaults to 10 for existing games, is saved/restored with the game, drives the action slider and bot size rounding, and is passed to pot splitting. A later cash configuration can choose 1 for cent precision (e.g. displayed 0.50/1.00 as 50/100 internal units).
- A UI or bot `raise` request's `amount` is **additional chips paid now**. `playerPatch.roundBet` is the **new street total**. The engine rejects malformed, fractional, below-minimum, above-stack, above-callable, and wrong-denomination raise amounts rather than silently changing them.
- The current actor is checked when `activeSeatIndex` is set. The acting player must belong to the table. This is not yet the future agent decision-ID and replay-protection boundary.
- A prior bettor cannot re-raise one insufficient all-in; two insufficient all-ins that cumulatively reach a full raise reopen betting. An unacted player may raise, and a prior checker can raise after an opening short all-in when another opponent can respond. Legal actions are capped by the amount another non-all-in player can contest. Existing tournament rules still have their other original constraints.

The raise-right rule follows [Poker TDA Rule 49 and examples](https://www.pokertda.com/view-poker-tda-rules/) and [WSOP live action no-limit rules 172–174](https://www.wsop.com/pdfs/2012/2012-Live-Action-Rules.pdf).

## Remaining gates

`C1/L1` must add configurable fixed blinds, buy-ins, top-ups, session accounting, and fully local resources. `O1` must add seat-private observations and decision IDs. `H1` must produce immutable hand histories. Existing tournament bots remain uncalibrated for cash play.
