# D0.1 Solver data, license, and coverage gate — 2026-09-30

> Historical preflop-source audit. The user's corrected priority is postflop solving; the controlling D0 decision and D1/D2 scope are now in `v1.2-d0-postflop-solver-feasibility.md`. This document remains as source research for possible starting ranges, not the selected feature plan.

## Decision

**Go only for a narrowly labeled short-stack push/fold lookup; No-Go for a per-hand or whole-table GTO grade.** The first source with an explicit *data* license is HoldemMath. It covers no-ante, no-rake, first-in shove/call decisions at selected 4–20 BB depths for heads-up and 6-max. Its 6-max model is a single-caller approximation. This can support a deliberately limited D1/D2 evaluation if the exact action line and stack depth match. It does not cover ordinary 100 BB cash hands, 3–5 seats, limps, non-all-in raises, or postflop play.

There is a more relevant 100 BB heads-up blueprint and several postflop solver engines, but they do **not** clear a general integration gate today. The blueprint's repository has an MIT software license, but its generated asset rights are not separately stated; its manifest reports no final exploitability value, and the data gives action frequencies rather than a verified EV for every alternative action. Postflop engines need two defensible ranges and an action tree; they do not turn a saved hand into an automatically solved, multiway game. No solver or strategy data was copied into this project in D0.

“Assess every hand” must mean a review page can list every human decision and say whether a validated reference covers it. A number called GTO score or EV loss is allowed only when a matching, validated source supplies comparable per-action EVs. Winning or losing the pot, bot reactions, raw hand equity, and the frequency of an action in a chart do not establish that EV loss.

## Product and history boundary

This is a private, offline, no-rake, no-ante, 2–6-seat NLHE cash trainer. The session buy-in is 20–500 BB, with 100 BB used by the current similar-spot setup. Browser runtime is plain JavaScript without new dependencies. `createHandHistory` records blinds, seat order, starting stacks, rake = 0, public actions, board, hero cards, and final payouts. `projectSeatHandHistory` removes unrevealed opponent cards. `replayHandHistory(history, action.seq - 1)` can reconstruct the visible pot, stacks, street bets, board, and folds just before a recorded action. `action.applied` is recorded after the decision, so the preceding event prefix is the correct information boundary. See `js/shared/handHistory.js`, `js/shared/handReview.js`, and the P2.0 data audit.

The history has no opponent range, solver bet tree, convergence evidence, counterfactual response, or archived decision-time bot memory. An opponent's later revealed cards must not be fed into an earlier decision evaluation. A posthoc solver can supply *conditional* advice from explicit ranges and assumptions; it cannot reveal the true EV against this particular bot hand from the player's information at decision time.

## D1/D2 scope allowed by D0

1. Build a decision-level coverage check from the hero-visible event prefix. Its result should record source ID/version, game assumptions (seats, blinds, ante, rake, stack depth, positions, action path, allowed sizes), hand class, and `exact / approximate / unavailable`. Here `exact` can only mean exact **scenario lookup**, never proof of exact GTO. HoldemMath's model result is `approximate` even when the lookup key matches because its equities are sampled and six-max strategy is approximated.
2. The first data-backed case may be **heads-up 20 BB, no-ante, no-rake, first-in shove or facing shove**, using the CC BY HoldemMath data. Six-max can be shown later only with its single-caller approximation prominently identified. Other table sizes, depths between published points, limps, raises, postflop actions, and unmatched lines return `unavailable`. Do not silently round 17 BB to 15 or 20 BB.
3. Display source frequency and the model's assumptions; do not calculate EV loss or a hand score unless both chosen-action and best-action EVs are available under the same tree and validated. The HoldemMath `e` field is strategy EV per hand class, not the complete alternate-action EV vector.
4. A useful 100 BB next step is a *separate* validation spike for the pinned HU blueprint: resolve asset rights, confirm rules and no-rake mechanics in the solver code, compare a few exact paths with independently regenerated solutions, and measure whether the asset can be consumed without adding runtime dependencies. If passed, it can provide HU preflop frequencies, still with no unproven EV-loss number. Postflop needs source-backed preflop ranges, an explicit bet tree, resource limits, and a separate license/architecture decision. Multiway postflop remains unavailable.

## Remaining gaps and acceptance checks

- No source reviewed supplies validated decision EVs for every action in ordinary 2–6-seat, 100 BB cash hands. There is no legitimate “grade every hand” claim yet.
- Audit eligibility using real completed histories: matched HU shove/call, unmatched 100 BB HU open, and a three-way flop. The last two must be `unavailable` under the first source pack.
- Before shipping third-party data: pin an immutable revision and file hash, keep its license/attribution with the asset and UI, verify row/action frequencies and card-class mapping, and check that every reported result names its assumptions.
- The upstream 100 BB blueprint manifest's SHA is for the **decompressed JSON** (the compressed gzip hash differs); this was verified on the pinned 100 BB no-ante shard. `final_exploitability_bb100` is `null`, so convergence must not be claimed from this manifest alone.

## Verification performed

- Inspected local hand-history projection/replay and the cash action recording path; no application logic changed.
- Inspected upstream primary READMEs, licenses, manifest, and one 100 BB blueprint shard at pinned revisions. The shard's decompressed SHA-256 matched its manifest entry; the compressed file's byte length matched `file_size_bytes`.
- No external solver was executed, no dataset was bundled, and no measured coverage rate or solver accuracy is asserted.
