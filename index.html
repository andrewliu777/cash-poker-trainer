    How this table works

    Enter your name to play against bots, or choose Watch 6 bots from Menu to spectate.

    Choose your setup

    Solo vs Bots (1 human)

    One human plays directly on the main device. No QR scan is required and that player’s hole cards remain visible on the table.

    Empty seats are filled by bots. This local version does not connect to the original remote table service.

    Spectator / TV Poker (0 humans)

    All seats are filled by bots. The table runs automatically and all hole cards remain visible.

    Before you start

    • Set an even big blind; the small blind is half of it. Choose a buy-in depth in BB and a between-hand top-up threshold.
    • Enter your name in one seat's name badge before starting a practice session.
    • Choose Menu → Watch 6 bots to start a spectator session.
    • Leave a seat empty if you want it filled by a bot.
    • Use the close icon to remove seats you do not need.
    • Keep 2–6 active seats. The table uses no rake.

    During a hand

    • Dealer and blinds are assigned automatically. Blind amounts stay fixed for the session.
    • The table uses standard poker action labels: Fold, Check, Call, Raise, All-in.
    • The slider stops at the largest amount another player can contest.
    • Pot size shortcuts set a bet or raise amount without submitting it. When facing a bet, they add the call before sizing the raise against the pot after calling; sizes below the legal minimum are unavailable.
    • Raises that are too small turn red while dragging and snap to the minimum legal raise when released.
    • Start the next hand from the table. If you run out of chips with automatic top-up off, confirm the top-up in the prompt. Open Menu for other top-ups, pause or resume, and ending a session. During a hand, ending takes effect after that hand. Top-ups are separate from winnings.

    Read the table

    • The pot is shown in the center. Community cards are in the middle, and each seat displays its stack and current round bet.
    • Dealer, small blind, and big blind icons indicate the current positions.
    • The notification bar is clickable and opens the hand log.
    • Open Menu for hand history, replay, statistics, and the action log. Statistics and the log become available after completed hands.
    • Win percentages and short hand labels only appear when hole cards are visible on the shared table, such as in spectator mode or during all-in runouts.
