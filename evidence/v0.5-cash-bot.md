# v0.5 / B1 cash bot gate

Date: 2026-09-30. Base: `3eafd40` (v0.4/H1). This is a private local trainer; the bot is an opponent, not a strategy reference.

## What changed

- The seat observation now carries the game mode. Before B1, cash sessions still passed through the tournament bot path because the mode was lost at this boundary. This also means the frozen Q1 fixed-spot enumeration exercised tournament decisions despite its cash labels; its results remain preserved as a baseline rather than being silently reinterpreted.
- Cash decisions use effective BB depth. Tournament M zones, elimination penalties, chip-rank pressure, blind-level adjustments, and the bot's three-raise ceiling do not determine cash actions. The engine's raise-right and amount checks still apply.
- The cash bot uses the original dealt table size and position when earlier seats fold. First-in cash sizing remains in integer chip units. Deep 5-bets can be smaller than all-in, while high-raise ranges narrow as action escalates. Free checks no longer produce `call 0`.
- The heads-up small blind plays a wider, depth-dependent first-in range. Multiway pure-air continuation bets and repeated raises with vulnerable paired-board hands are reduced. These are rule heuristics, not solver outputs.

## Independent opponent gate

The [opponent pool script](b1-opponent-pool.mjs) freezes two transparent rule opponents: tight value and loose pressure. They use their own cards and public board only. Each condition plays both bot seats over paired seeds, grouped into 60 blocks of 100 hands per seat. The interval is `mean ± 1.96 × block standard error`, an indicative measure for this pool, not proof of performance against people.

Two earlier unseen seeds exposed real failures. [Seed 20270111](b1-opponent-pool-20270111.json) showed negative results against loose pressure before the paired-board fixes. [Seed 20270201](b1-opponent-pool-20270201.json) again showed negative 20/200BB results. Development diagnostics then found two repeated mechanisms: weak paired-board hands escalating large pots, and heads-up SB folding roughly half of first-in hands. Both were changed before the final unseen seed was run. Those failed results remain recorded and were not reused as a passing holdout.

## Decision and remaining boundary

B1 passes as a cash-aware **practice opponent** for the tested local 2–6 seat rules and 20–200BB probes. Its actions must not be presented as GTO answers, and this gate says nothing about real-player strength, rake, 9-max, or untested strategies. Independent win-rate evidence is heads-up at 20/100/200BB; 40/60/150BB and multiway were checked for legality and distributions, not benchmarked against independent strategy opponents.

The next DAG node is G1: integrate the v0.5 bot with the saved-hand UI, run browser and offline acceptance, verify restores and exports after bot-led cash hands, and make a recoverable local backup before marking v1.0 ready for ongoing practice.
