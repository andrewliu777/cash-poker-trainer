# P2.2 Grouped hand stats — 2026-09-30

## Result

The Statistics view now reads completed local and restored hands, deduplicates them through the existing history loader, and groups the human seat by free table or similar sample, dealer-relative position, and starting depth. Depth buckets are `<40 BB`, `40–99 BB`, and `100+ BB`. Each mode has its own total and breakdown rows. A hand's result is its ending stack minus its starting stack, divided by that hand's big blind; the displayed Hand Δ is the sum of those per-hand BB results. It is not a bankroll or a strategy grade.

VPIP means the human paid voluntarily before the flop; forced blinds do not count. PFR means a preflop raise or an all-in above the prior largest street bet. The opportunity denominator counts completed hands in which the human had at least one preflop action. A big-blind walk therefore shows `0/0 (–)` for both actions. Counts and denominators are shown beside percentages. Existing current-table player totals remain in a disclosure below the grouped view.
