# O1 observation boundary — 2026-09-29

The browser, browserless batch runner, and structural smoke scripts now project a seat-specific observation before calling the legacy bot. The projection contains the acting seat's hole cards and public table fields. It does not contain other hole cards, the deck, discarded cards, all-knowing win probabilities, UI visibility flags, or other agents' internal `botLine` memory. The legacy bot receives a copy of its own memory and returns the next copy.

Checks run:

- 68/68 engine, bot normalization, and observation tests passed under Node's test shim.
- Replacing opponent hole cards, their UI visibility, their win probability, their internal plan, and future cards did not change the acting seat's observation or legacy bot decision under the same random stream.
- `node evidence/cash-smoke.mjs`: 1,000 six-seat cash hands, 88 top-ups, fixed blinds and conserved chips.
- `node evidence/bot-smoke.mjs`: 100 seeded tournaments, 9,046 hands, conserved chips.

This was the first part of O1. The completed O1 evidence is in `v0.3-info-boundary.md`. The strategy remains tournament-oriented and its cash-game quality is not yet established.
