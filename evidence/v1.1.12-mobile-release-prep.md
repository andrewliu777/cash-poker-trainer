- Added first preflop re-raise presets to three or four times the open amount. The amount is a **raise-to total**, with a previously posted blind subtracted from the hero's payment.

## Release boundary

This is an **R0 local preparation**, not a cloud release. The app currently stores completed hands and annotations in per-origin IndexedDB and an unfinished session in localStorage. Separate browsers do not sync. A public HTTPS link alone would give separate local records, but would not meet the user's new requirement that one account share a live session and history across computer and phone.

R1 must define account identity, per-user local namespacing and migration, authenticated cloud storage, and conflict handling before opening a multi-user public link. In particular, the existing unscoped local database and saved-game key cannot be reused unchanged when accounts can be switched in one browser profile. R2–R4 must pass the cross-device and cross-account gates in the roadmap before the release is called stable.
